## FAQ Section

**Headline:** Catering FAQs

**What's your minimum order?**  
We typically cater events for **10 or more guests**, depending on menu and service style. Contact us with your details and we'll confirm what we can do.

**What's your service area?**  
We primarily serve **Knoxville, TN and the surrounding area**. For locations farther out, we can discuss travel and logistics.

**Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?**  
Yes. We design **personalized menus** that can include vegetarian, pork-free, seafood-free, and other options. Tell us your needs in the inquiry form and we'll work with you.

**How far in advance should we book?**  
We recommend **as early as possible**, especially for weddings and large events. For smaller events, a few weeks' notice is often enough.

**Do you offer tastings?**  
For weddings and larger events, we can arrange **tastings** so you can try dishes before committing. Details and any tasting fees can be discussed during your consultation.
