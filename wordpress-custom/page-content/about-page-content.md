## Why She Started Sweet n' Sassy

**Headline:** Why Sweet n' Sassy — Bringing Authentic Creole Soul Food to Knoxville

**Body:**  
Sweet n' Sassy was born from a simple goal: give Knoxville access to the best tasting, most authentic Creole Soul food — the kind that comes from training, tradition, and a genuine love for the craft. Chef Tandalyn wanted a place where quality and hospitality weren't optional; where every guest could taste the difference that 30+ years of experience makes. The food truck and catering business became the way to bring that vision to markets, events, and tables across the city.

## Philosophy: Quality, Precision, Exceptional Experiences

**Headline:** Our Philosophy — Quality, Precision, Exceptional Experiences

**Body:**  
For Chef Tandalyn and the Sweet n' Sassy team, three things guide everything we do:

- **Quality** — We source and prepare food with care. Every dish is something we're proud to serve.
- **Precision** — Technique matters. Our training and experience go into every roux, every grit, every plate.
- **Exceptional experiences** — Whether you're at the truck window or at a wedding reception, we want your experience to be memorable. Great food and warm, professional service are the standard.

That's our brand promise: the best tasting food with exceptional culinary experiences, every time.
