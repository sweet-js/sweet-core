## Finding the Truck

**Headline:** How to Find Us

1. **Check this week's schedule** above for locations and times.
2. **Tap "Get Directions"** next to the stop you want — your maps app will open with the address.
3. **Look for the Sweet n' Sassy truck** and our signage.
4. **Order at the window** — same menu, same quality, same Chef Tandalyn standards.

**Tip:** Popular items (including our signature Shrimp and Grits) can sell out. Arriving earlier in the service window helps ensure you get what you want.
