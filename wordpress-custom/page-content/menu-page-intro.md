## Signature Dish Spotlight: Shrimp and Grits

**Headline:** Our Signature Dish — Shrimp and Grits

**Description:**  
Chef Tandalyn's Shrimp and Grits is the heart of the Sweet n' Sassy menu. Plump Gulf shrimp rest on creamy, stone-ground grits, finished with a sauce that's rich, slightly spicy, and unmistakably Creole. It's the dish that defines us: best tasting, expertly prepared, and made with the same care we've brought to the table for 30+ years.

**Callout:** *Ask for it at the truck or add it to your catering menu — it's the one dish we're known for.*

## Category Descriptions

### Appetizers

Start with something that shows off our craft: boudin balls, fried green tomatoes, crawfish beignets. Every bite is prepared with the same precision as our main dishes.

### Entrees

From jambalaya and seafood gumbo to po' boys, étouffée, and red beans & rice — our entrees are where Chef Tandalyn's training and Creole roots shine. Hearty, flavorful, and made to be remembered.

### Sides

Mac & cheese, collard greens, cornbread, dirty rice. Our sides are cooked with care and meant to complement every plate. Quality in every scoop.

### Desserts

Beignets, bread pudding with bourbon sauce, pralines. Sweet finishes that round out the Creole Soul experience — no shortcuts, just great taste.

### Beverages

Sweet tea, fresh lemonade, Abita root beer, and more. The perfect match for a hot plate and a warm day in Knoxville.

### Catering Packages

Build your event menu from the same dishes we serve on the truck — with personalized options and the quality you expect from Chef Tandalyn and the Sweet n' Sassy team.
